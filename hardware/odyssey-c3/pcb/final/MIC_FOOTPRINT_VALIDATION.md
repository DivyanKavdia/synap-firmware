# MIC1 footprint validation — Rev K

The exact breakout-manufacturer tolerance drawing has not been located, so Rev K does not claim 7.62 mm is manufacturer-certified.

A straight-on supplied image, calibrated using the stated 14 mm module diameter, gives approximate:
- horizontal pad pitch: 2.55 mm
- row-to-row spacing: 7.68 mm

This strongly supports the nominal 2.54 mm / 7.62 mm geometry, but remains image-derived.

Rev K therefore uses a tolerance-friendly PTH:
- centres: 2.54 mm horizontal, 7.62 mm row spacing
- plated drill: 1.40 mm
- copper pad: 2.10 mm
- annular ring: 0.35 mm
- adjacent-pad copper gap: 0.44 mm

A 1:1 physical fit template is included. Print at 100% / no scaling and place the actual module/header on it before volume fabrication.
